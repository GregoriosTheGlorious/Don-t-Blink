Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what DON’T BLINK (“the game”, at dontblink.ink) stores and shares. It is a small game: no accounts, no emails, no purchases.

Stored on your device

The game saves a few items in your browser’s local storage so it can remember you: your best score, your sound setting, the nickname you chose for the leaderboard, a random device ID, and a note of which scores you have already posted. These stay on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Leaderboard

If you post a score, we store your nickname, score, the number of rounds and time played, the random device ID, a timestamp, and a one-way hash of your network (IP) address that we use only to limit spam and cheating. We do not store your name, email or exact location. Nicknames and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard, so please do not use a real name or personal information as your nickname. The leaderboard data is processed by Supabase, our database provider.

Hosting

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Advertising

We may show ads on the start screen served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, or opt out of many third-party ad vendors at aboutads.info. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can clear the data stored on your device at any time in your browser settings. To ask us to delete a leaderboard entry, contact us with your nickname and score.
