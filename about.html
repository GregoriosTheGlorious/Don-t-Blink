DON’T BLINK

A one-tap timing game. A dot circles the ring. Tap anywhere to stop it inside the green zone. Hit and the next round is faster and tighter. Miss and the eye blinks, and the game is over.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere (or press Space) to stop the dot. It has to land inside the green zone.
  • Perfect hits, dead centre on the white line, score more and build a streak.
  • Streak and shield: three perfects in a row earn a shield that absorbs one miss.
  • Rounds vary: every five rounds the zone sizes are shuffled, so a tight zone is usually followed by an easier one.
  • Scoring: 10 points for a hit, 20 to 45 for a perfect depending on your streak.

Leaderboard

Choose a nickname after a game to post your score to the Today and All-time boards. No account is needed. See the privacy policy for what is stored.

About

DON’T BLINK is a small independent game made for the browser. It works on phones and desktops, and you can add it to your home screen to play like an app.
